About

PhD applicant with a first-ranked M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and research experience in Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning, mathematical optimization, and intelligent decision-making for complex dynamical systems.

My research has focused on learning-based control and coordination in energy and autonomous multi-agent systems. In power systems, I have worked on distributed energy resources — smart PV inverters, battery energy storage systems, and electric vehicle charging systems — addressing voltage regulation, peak demand management, loss reduction, energy arbitrage, and cyber-physical resilience under uncertain operating conditions. I have also developed learning-based frameworks for autonomous UAV swarms, focused on decentralized navigation, collision avoidance, and task allocation under limited communication.

My research involves deep reinforcement learning, evolutionary and probabilistic optimization, and scenario-based modelling, with practical experience in Python, MATLAB, OpenDSS, and ROS — a foundation for formulating, implementing, and evaluating data-driven methods for dynamic multi-agent systems. My broader background includes centralized training with decentralized execution, thermal-aware energy management, and safe decision-making under false data injection attacks.
